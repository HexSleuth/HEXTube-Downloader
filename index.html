How this works: 100% client-side — no server, no backend. The app queries free public Piped and Invidious API instances (automatic failover across ~20 mirrors) to get proxied stream URLs, then downloads the chosen file directly to your browser. Muxed MP4 (typically 360p / 720p) and audio-only streams download here. For 1080p+ and merged output, use the Open in ytdlnis handoff (Android) — it uses the installed app instead.
