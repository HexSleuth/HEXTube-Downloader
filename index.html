Formats supported
  • Video: MP4 / H.264 — resolution follows the source (up to 4K on merged Cobalt streams).
  • Audio: MP3, M4A, Opus, OGG, WAV, WebM.
  • Multi-item posts (carousels, slideshows, playlists) expose a picker so you can grab any item.
What changed in this build
  • Cobalt 404 fixed — the old /api/json endpoint is gone; only POST / on the base URL is used, matching current Cobalt v10.
  • Auto-detect — working Piped, Invidious and Cobalt instances are discovered on first load and refreshed on demand.
  • Multi-instance failover — each download tries the detected list in order; your manual override always goes first.
Limits Direct YouTube streams top out around 128–160 kbps audio and 360p/720p muxed video. For merged 1080p+/4K or exotic containers (AVI, FLV, WMV, 3GP, M4V) use yt-dlp with ffmpeg.
